What Australian buyers should watch for

When shopping for a television in Australia, energy rating labels and standby consumption matter just as much as picture quality. Larger screens often draw more power, but eco modes, automatic brightness control and lower peak brightness settings can reduce consumption without significantly affecting enjoyment. The best long-term choice balances performance with efficient operation over the life of the appliance.
