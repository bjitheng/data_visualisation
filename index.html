Why appliance energy matters

⚡

Household demand

TVs, consoles and media devices remain active for hours each day, making standby settings and efficient mode selection relevant for reducing annual power use.

📊

Market trends

The Australian market is moving toward larger screens, smarter automation and lower power consumption, creating better options for consumers and lower running costs over time.

🌱

Environmental outcome

Lower appliance consumption helps reduce emissions intensity in electricity generation and supports more sustainable homes across Australian cities and regional communities.

Placeholder insight for the Australian market

In Australia, appliance energy use is shaped by climate, household size and the increasing use of connected entertainment systems. Modern televisions are now designed to balance brightness, motion smoothing and smart platform activity, all of which influence daily electricity draw. Consumers can lower consumption by choosing energy-efficient star-rated models and reducing screen brightness when possible.
